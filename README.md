# SB Plant Breakdown

**Designed and built by Emilie Ngo for Succulents Box.**

A sales analysis app that collects sales data on a schedule and shows how individual products, categories, and vendors are performing.

## Features

- Compare sales across channels and time periods.
- See top sellers and the biggest sales increases and declines.
- Review vendor performance and open the underlying product breakdown.
- Export the breakdown for further analysis.

Product reporting is also available in the [Marketing Dashboard](https://github.com/emilie-afk/sb-marketing-dashboard).
