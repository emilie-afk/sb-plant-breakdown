Catalog

The storefront catalog (/products.json) is fetched automatically and cached for 24 hours. Sales rows are matched to it by product id, otherwise by a unique exact title and vendor; ambiguous rows need an approved mapping, kept under Shared settings (catalog_mappings). Product-level sales are never split across variants.

Troubleshooting

If a collection failed: make sure the collector PC is on at the scheduled time and signed in to Shopify (run npm run login in collector/ once if asked), then press Refresh. A failed run never replaces the last successful report.
